🕉 Kundli FAQ

Chart Basics

What is a Kundli?

▾

A Kundli (also called Janam Kundali) is a Vedic birth chart. It is a map of the sky at the exact moment of your birth, showing where each planet was positioned among the 12 zodiac signs and 12 houses. It is used to understand personality, life events, and timing.

Why does birth time matter so much?

▾

The Ascendant (Lagna) changes roughly every 2 hours. Even a few minutes difference can shift the Lagna to a different sign, which changes the entire house structure. Accurate birth time is essential for a reliable chart.

What is the difference between Vedic and Western astrology?

▾

Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac (based on actual star positions), while Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac (based on seasons). The difference is about 24 degrees, called Ayanamsa. This means your Vedic sign may differ from your Western sign.

Why use Vedic astrology instead of Western?

▾

Vedic (Jyotish) astrology tracks actual star positions using the sidereal zodiac. Western astrology uses the tropical zodiac tied to seasons. Due to precession, these systems have drifted apart by about 24 degrees, so your Vedic Sun sign may be one sign earlier than your Western sign. Vedic astrology also uses the Dasha system for timing predictions, Nakshatras (27 lunar mansions) for deeper personality analysis, and divisional charts for specific life areas. Neither system is better. They offer different perspectives. Many practitioners use both.

Can astrology predict my career?

▾

Your 10th house, its lord, and planets placed there indicate career tendencies. The current dasha period shows which professional themes are active. Generate your Kundli and check the Career life area or ask the chatbot about your career for a detailed analysis.

Matching

What is a good Gun Milan score?

▾

The total is out of 36. Below 18 is generally not recommended. 18 to 24 is average. 24 to 32 is good. Above 32 is excellent. However, the total alone does not tell the full story. Check individual Koota scores and doshas too.

Does a low score mean the marriage will fail?

▾

No. Many successful marriages have low Gun Milan scores. Compatibility is built through communication, mutual respect, and shared values. The score is one input, not a verdict. Use it as guidance, not a decision maker.

When will I get married?

▾

Marriage timing depends on the 7th house lord's dasha period, Venus and Jupiter transits, and the overall strength of the 7th house. Generate your Kundli and ask the chatbot "when will I get married?" for a personalized timing analysis based on your dasha periods.

Does person order matter in matching?

▾

Yes. In traditional Ashtakoota matching, Person 1's Varna is compared against Person 2's. If Person 1's Varna is equal to or higher than Person 2's, full points are awarded. Swapping the order may change the Varna score by 1 point.

Doshas

Am I Manglik? What does it mean?

▾

You are Manglik if Mars is in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12 in your birth chart. It is traditionally considered significant for marriage compatibility. However, the effects reduce after age 28, and if both partners are Manglik, the dosha is neutralized.

What is Sade Sati?

▾

Sade Sati is a 7.5-year period when Saturn transits through the 12th, 1st, and 2nd signs from your natal Moon. It is a period of challenges that builds resilience, discipline, and lasting achievements. It occurs roughly every 30 years.

Which gemstone should I wear?

▾

The right gemstone depends on which planet you want to strengthen. Generally, strengthen your Lagna lord or the lord of a beneficial house. Never wear Blue Sapphire or Cat's Eye without expert consultation. Generate your chart to see your planetary strengths, then consult an astrologer for personalized gemstone advice.

Dasha

What is a Mahadasha?

▾

A Mahadasha is a major planetary period in the Vimshottari Dasha system. Each planet rules for a specific number of years (Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, etc.). The total cycle is 120 years. Your current Mahadasha lord influences your life themes.

What are Antardasha and Pratyantar?

▾

Each Mahadasha is subdivided into 9 Antardashas (sub-periods), and each Antardasha into 9 Pratyantars (sub-sub-periods). These finer divisions help pinpoint timing of events more precisely.

Remedies

Do remedies actually work?

▾

Remedies are a traditional practice meant to channel planetary energy constructively. Whether through mantras, gemstones, or charitable acts, the core idea is mindful action aligned with the planet's nature. Many people find them helpful as a focus for positive intention.

Should I wear a gemstone without consulting an astrologer?

▾

Gemstones amplify a planet's energy. If the planet is well-placed, this is beneficial. If poorly placed, it can increase challenges. It is best to consult a qualified astrologer before wearing gemstones, especially Blue Sapphire (Neelam) and Cat's Eye (Lehsunia).

Technical

What Ayanamsa does this app use?

▾

This app uses Lahiri (Chitrapaksha) Ayanamsa, which is the most widely used system in India and the standard recommended by the Indian government's calendar reform committee.

What house system is used?

▾

This app uses the Placidus house system for calculating house cusps. This is the most common system in both Vedic and Western astrology software. Note that Placidus can be unreliable at extreme latitudes (above 66 degrees).
