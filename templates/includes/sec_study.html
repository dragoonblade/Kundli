What is a Kundli?

Foundations of Vedic astrology

▾
Concept

A Kundli is a map of the sky at the exact moment you were born, as seen from your birthplace. It shows where each planet was positioned among the 12 zodiac signs and 12 houses.

Vedic astrology uses the sidereal zodiac (based on actual star positions), not the tropical zodiac used in Western astrology. The difference is called Ayanamsa, currently about 24 degrees.

Your Chart

Your chart was cast for {{ birth_dt.strftime('%d %B %Y at %I:%M %p') }}. Your Ascendant (Lagna) is {{ lagna.sign }} at {{ "%.2f"|format(lagna.degree) }} degrees. This is the sign that was rising on the eastern horizon at your birth. It shapes how the world sees you.

Key Takeaway

Your Kundli is unique to your birth time and place. Even twins born minutes apart can have different charts. Birth time accuracy matters.

The 12 Signs (Rashis)

Characteristics of each zodiac sign

▾
Concept

The zodiac is divided into 12 signs of 30 degrees each. Each sign has an element (fire, earth, air, water), a quality (cardinal, fixed, mutable), and a ruling planet.

Fire signs (Mesha, Simha, Dhanu) are energetic. Earth signs (Vrishabha, Kanya, Makara) are practical. Air signs (Mithuna, Tula, Kumbha) are intellectual. Water signs (Karka, Vrishchika, Meena) are emotional.

Your Signs

Your Lagna (Ascendant) is {{ lagna.sign }}. This is your outward personality.

Your Moon sign is {{ (planets|selectattr('planet', 'equalto', 'Chandra')|first).sign }}. This is your emotional nature and inner self.

Your Sun sign is {{ (planets|selectattr('planet', 'equalto', 'Surya')|first).sign }}. This is your core identity and ego.

Key Takeaway

In Vedic astrology, the Moon sign is more important than the Sun sign. Your Moon sign determines your Nakshatra, Dasha starting point, and emotional temperament.

The 9 Planets (Navagraha)

What each planet represents

▾
Concept

Vedic astrology uses 9 celestial bodies: Sun, Moon, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Saturn, plus Rahu and Ketu (the lunar nodes). Each planet governs specific life areas.

Benefics (Guru, Shukra, Budh) generally bring positive results. Malefics (Shani, Mangal, Rahu, Ketu) bring challenges that build character. Surya and Chandra are luminaries with mixed effects.

A planet is strongest when exalted (in its best sign) and weakest when debilitated. When in its own sign, it is comfortable and effective.

Key Takeaway

Each planet colors the sign it sits in. A planet in a friendly sign works well. A planet in an enemy sign struggles. Check which of your planets are in strong or weak positions.

The 12 Houses (Bhavas)

What each house governs in your life

▾
Concept

The 12 houses represent different areas of life. House 1 is self, House 7 is marriage, House 10 is career, and so on. The sign on each house cusp and the planets inside determine how that area plays out.

Kendras (1, 4, 7, 10) are the pillars of your chart. Trikonas (1, 5, 9) bring luck and merit. Dusthanas (6, 8, 12) bring challenges but also transformation.

Key Takeaway

To read any house: check the sign on the cusp, find its ruling planet, see where that ruler sits. That connection tells the story. For example, your 7th house sign's ruler placement reveals marriage dynamics.

Aspects (Drishti)

How planets influence each other

▾
Concept

In Vedic astrology, every planet aspects the 7th house from itself (the opposite sign). Mars also aspects the 4th and 8th. Jupiter aspects the 5th and 9th. Saturn aspects the 3rd and 10th. These are called special aspects.

{% endfor %}
Key Takeaway

Aspects extend a planet's influence beyond its own house. A planet can affect a house it does not sit in. Always check aspects when reading any house.

Sign Lordship

How houses connect through rulers

▾
Concept

Each sign has a ruling planet (lord). The lord of a house connects that house to wherever the lord sits. If your 7th lord sits in the 10th house, marriage is connected to career. This is how astrologers trace life patterns.

Lordship is the backbone of prediction. Where a house lord sits tells you where that house's energy flows. Trace the chain for any house to understand its full story.

The Dasha System

Timing predictions with planetary periods

▾
Concept

Vimshottari Dasha divides your life into 9 planetary periods totaling 120 years. The starting point is determined by your Moon's nakshatra at birth. Each planet's period activates the houses it rules and occupies.

Your Dasha

You are currently in {{ current_dasha }} ({{ planet_names[current_dasha] }}) Mahadasha.

This means the houses ruled by {{ current_dasha }} in your chart are especially active right now. Look at where {{ current_dasha }} sits and what houses it rules to understand your current life themes.

Key Takeaway

The chart shows potential. The dasha shows timing. A strong planet gives its best results during its own dasha period. This is how astrologers predict when events will happen.

Doshas and Remedies

Challenges and how to address them

▾
Concept

A dosha is an area of intensity in your chart. Manglik Dosha (Mars in houses 1, 2, 4, 7, 8, or 12) affects marriage. Kalsarpa Dosha (all planets between Rahu and Ketu) indicates a focused karmic path. Sade Sati is Saturn transiting near your Moon.

Doshas are not curses. They are areas that need awareness and sometimes remedial action.

11

Divisional Charts

Going deeper with Navamsa and beyond

▾
Concept

The birth chart (D1) is the main chart. Divisional charts zoom into specific life areas. Navamsa (D9) is the most important. It reveals the deeper truth about marriage, dharma, and inner strength. Dasamsa (D10) shows career specifics.

Each divisional chart divides each sign into smaller parts. In Navamsa, each sign is split into 9 parts of 3 degrees 20 minutes each.

Your Navamsa

Compare your D1 (birth chart) planet positions with the Navamsa positions in the Full Chart view. If a planet is strong in both D1 and D9, its results are confirmed. If weak in D9, the D1 promise may not fully deliver.

Key Takeaway

D1 shows the promise. D9 confirms or denies it. Always check Navamsa for marriage questions and D10 for career questions.

Reading a Complete Chart

Putting it all together

▾
The Full Process

1. Note the Lagna sign and its lord placement. This sets the foundation.

2. Check Moon sign and nakshatra for emotional nature and dasha starting point.

3. See which houses have planets. Those areas are most active in life.

4. Trace lordship chains for key houses (1st, 7th, 10th) to understand self, marriage, career.

5. Check aspects, especially from Saturn, Jupiter, and Mars.

6. Identify yogas for special strengths or challenges.

7. Check doshas for areas needing attention.

8. Look at the current dasha to understand present life themes.

9. Confirm with Navamsa (D9) for marriage and D10 for career.
