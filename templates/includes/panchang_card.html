What is Panchang? ▾

Panchang means "five limbs" and tracks five daily astronomical elements: Tithi (lunar day), Nakshatra (Moon's star), Yoga (Sun-Moon combination), Karana (half-tithi), and Paksha (lunar phase). It is used to determine auspicious timing for important activities.
